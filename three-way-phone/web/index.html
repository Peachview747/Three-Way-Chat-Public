One conversation, your iPhone.

This is the AI Room dashboard you know, hosted by your phone. Cloudflare serves the interface and carries authenticated requests to the app. Your conversations are saved on the iPhone.

Keep the app open and connected to use the website. Your laptop can be off. The app reconnects when you reopen it.

GPT and Claude connections come later. This build saves your prompts with the recipients you choose; it does not send them to an assistant or generate replies.

Open your phone’s room.

On your iPhone, open AI Room Connector and tap How to set up and pair a browser. Import your private phone setup file and wait for Connected to Cloudflare. Under Chat website, share or copy the private website link and open it on this device to pair automatically.

To enter codes instead, expand Pair a browser with codes instead under Chat website in the iPhone app. Copy the room code and browser pairing code into the fields below. If you have no setup file, ask the person who manages your AI Room website for it.
